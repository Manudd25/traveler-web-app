# ✈️ Traveler

**Plan it. Book it. Live it.**

Traveler is an interactive travel planning web application designed to bring all parts of a trip together in one place.

From organizing an itinerary to managing a budget and exploring interactive booking experiences, Traveler turns trip planning into a more visual and engaging experience.

Live demo: https://traveler-web-app.surge.sh/

---

## 🌍 What can you do with Traveler?

### 🗺️ Plan your trip

Create and organize your journey with a day-by-day itinerary, destinations, activities, and important travel information.

---

### 🍽️ Choose your restaurant table

Instead of a traditional booking form, Traveler includes an interactive restaurant floor plan where users can select available tables and simulate a reservation.

---

### 🎬 Pick your cinema seat

Explore an interactive cinema layout, choose an available seat, and simulate the booking experience directly from the application.

---

### 🎟️ Book tours & activities

Explore activities and configure a booking by selecting options such as:

- 📅 Date and time  
- 👥 Number of participants  
- 🌐 Available language  
- 🧭 Activity or tour  

---

### 🤖 Meet Atlas

Atlas is Traveler’s AI travel guide, powered by Cloudflare Workers AI.

Users can chat with Atlas for help while planning and organizing their journey.

---

### 💰 Keep track of your budget

Traveler provides an overview of trip expenses and helps users monitor how much of their travel budget has already been used.

---

### ✅ Check your travel readiness

A travel checklist helps users see what has already been prepared and what still needs attention before departure.

---

### 📍 Explore your journey

Trip information, places, activities, and bookings are brought together to make the journey easier to understand and manage.

---

## ✨ The idea behind Traveler

Travel plans often end up scattered across booking platforms, notes, maps, emails, and different apps.

Traveler explores a different approach: creating one central place where users can **plan, organize, and interact with their journey**.

The project also experiments with more visual booking experiences, such as choosing a restaurant table or cinema seat instead of relying only on traditional forms.

## ⚠️ Demo Project

Traveler is a portfolio project.

Booking experiences inside the application are simulations and **do not create real-world reservations or purchases**.

---

## 🚀 Project Vision

The goal of Traveler is to explore how travel planning can become more visual, interactive, and connected — bringing itineraries, activities, bookings, and trip organization together in one experience.
